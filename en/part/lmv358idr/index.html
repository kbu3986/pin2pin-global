Engineering Overview

The Texas Instruments LMV358IDR is a low-voltage (2.7 V to 5.5 V), rail-to-rail output dual operational amplifier in an 8-pin SOIC (D) package rated from -40°C to +125°C. Built as a low-voltage counterpart to the classic LM358 on the exact same SOIC-8 pinout, it eliminates Class-B crossover distortion, boosts bandwidth to 1 MHz and slew rate to 1 V/µs, and cuts quiescent current to 105 µA/ch typ (210 µA total at 5 V). Designers must verify two hard constraints: its 5.5 V absolute maximum supply rating (zero headroom above 5.5 V) and non-rail-to-rail upper input limit (VCC+ - 1.0 V).

Critical Design Gotchas & Hardware Verification Notes

CRITICALDatasheet p.5

5.5 V Absolute Maximum Ceiling — Destructive If Swapped into LM358 High-Voltage Boards

Despite sharing the 358 designation and identical SOIC-8 pinout with LM358DR (32 V abs max), LMV358IDR has an absolute maximum supply rating of only 5.5 V with zero overvoltage headroom above its 5.5 V operating maximum. Installing it on a 9 V, 12 V, or 24 V LM358 board causes immediate permanent damage.

WARNINGDatasheet p.7

Rail-to-Rail Output Only — Input Common-Mode Stops 1.0 V Below VCC+

LMV358IDR provides rail-to-rail output swing (within 10 mV typ of VCC+ at RL = 10 kΩ), but its input common-mode range is only 0 V to 4.0 V at VCC+ = 5 V (0 V to 1.7 V at 2.7 V). Driving noninverting buffers near VCC+ clips or distorts the output; use TLV9002IDR for true RRIO.

WARNINGDatasheet p.6

High SOIC-8 Thermal Resistance (207.9 °C/W) Under Heavy Output Loads

TI updated LMV358 SOIC-8 RθJA to 207.9 °C/W (significantly higher than LM358DR at 124.7 °C/W). Continuous high output currents near the 40 mA short-circuit limit at elevated ambient temperatures (-40°C to 125°C) must be thermally derated to stay below TJ = 150°C.

WARNINGDatasheet p.3

Identical SOIC-8 Pinout to LM393DR Open-Collector Comparator

Shares the standard dual SOIC-8 footprint with the LM393DR comparator, but LMV358IDR is a compensated push-pull op-amp rated only to 5.5 V. Do not cross-populate with LM393DR without checking supply voltage, pull-up resistors, and compensation requirements.
