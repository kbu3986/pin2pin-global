Engineering Overview

The Texas Instruments TLV75733PDBVR is a 1 A, fixed 3.3 V (±1.5% over -40°C to +125°C; ±1.0% to +85°C), low-IQ (25 uA typ) PMOS LDO in a standard 5-pin DBV SOT-23-5 package (1=IN, 2=GND, 3=EN, 4=NC, 5=OUT) with 95 Ohm active output discharge and 1 uF–200 uF ceramic stability. Operating from 1.45 V to 5.5 V (6.0 V abs max) with 300 mV typ / 425 mV max dropout at 1 A (to 85°C), it is a strong high-current SOT-23-5 upgrade over TLV75533PDBVR when VIN - VOUT is small, but its 231.1 °C/W JEDEC RθJA (no thermal pad vs DYD variant), foldback startup limit, and lack of reverse-current blocking demand careful thermal and sequencing verification.

Critical Design Gotchas & Hardware Verification Notes

CRITICALDatasheet p.5

Severe 231.1 °C/W DBV Thermal Wall Blocks 1 A Load From 5 V Input (Use DYD Exposed-Pad Variant)

Despite its 1 A rating, the non-exposed-pad DBV SOT-23-5 package has JEDEC RθJA = 231.1 °C/W (p.5). Dropping 5.0 V to 3.3 V at 1 A dissipates 1.70 W—causing an impossible ΔT = 393 °C on JEDEC (171 °C even on TI's 100.8 °C/W EVM). Limit 5 V->3.3 V continuous load on DBV to <= 250 mA at 25 °C JEDEC, use a 3.8 V–4.0 V pre-regulator, or switch to the exposed-pad TLV75733PDYDR (60.3 °C/W EVM / 92.5 °C/W JEDEC).

WARNINGDatasheet p.13

Hybrid Foldback Current Limit (755 mA Typ) Can Stall Constant-Current Loads at Startup

Section 6.3.3 (p.13) activates foldback below 0.4 * VOUT(NOM), cutting available startup current toward 755 mA typ (well below the 1.2 A min regulated limit). Heavy constant-current loads or pre-biased negative outputs can latch the rail in foldback unless load enable is delayed until VOUT reaches regulation.

WARNINGDatasheet p.18

No Reverse-Current Blocking & EN > VIN Leakage / 10 kHz Toggle Limit

The PMOS body diode conducts unblocked reverse current whenever VOUT > VIN + 0.3 V (Sec. 7.1.4, p.18); add an external Schottky diode across OUT-to-IN if COUT is large and VIN can collapse. Although EN survives 6.0 V independent of VIN, driving EN above VIN increases EN pin current (Fig. 5-24) and restricts EN toggle rate to <= 10 kHz (p.12–13).

INFODatasheet p.7

3.3 V Output Noise Scales to ~151 uVRMS (2.1x Higher Than Tabulated 1.2 V Value)

Table 5.5 (p.6) lists 71.5 uVRMS noise at VOUT = 1.2 V, whereas Figure 5-6 (p.7) shows output noise rises linearly with VOUT to ~151 uVRMS at 3.3 V (10 Hz–100 kHz). For ultra-low-noise RF/ADC rails on the same SOT-23-5 footprint, consider TPS7A2033PDBVR (~6.2 uVRMS).

Typical Engineering Applications

  • High-current (up to 1 A) low-headroom 3.8 V–4.2 V to 3.3 V post-regulation rails in space-constrained SOT-23-5 layouts
  • Pin-compatible 1 A upgrade path over 500 mA TLV75533PDBVR or 300 mA TLV73333PDBVR when PCB copper supports heat dissipation
  • Wi-Fi/BLE/cellular burst-load 3.3 V supplies needing 1.2 A min current limit and monotonic internal soft-start
  • Battery-backed 3.3 V subsystems requiring 25 uA typ Iq, 0.1 uA shutdown, and 95 Ohm active output discharge
