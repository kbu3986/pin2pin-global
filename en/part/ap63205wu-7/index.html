Engineering Overview

The Diodes Incorporated AP63205WU-7 is a 3.8 V to 32 V input (35 V DC / 40 V 400 ms abs max), 2 A continuous fixed 5.0 V (±1%) synchronous buck converter in a 6-pin TSOT-23 (TSOT26) package running at 1.1 MHz with ±6% FSS, 22 uA PFM IQ, 35 V-tolerant float-to-enable precision EN, and ~94.5% peak efficiency at 12 V->5 V (4.7 uH inductor, 2x22 uF ceramic COUT). Because Pin 1 (FB) ties directly to 5.0 V (abs max 6.0 V) and its pinout (1=FB, 2=EN, 3=VIN, 4=GND, 5=SW, 6=BST) is incompatible with TI SOT-23-6 bucks and AP62200/AP62300, never confuse marking 'T5' (5 V) with pin-identical 'T4' AP63203 (3.3 V) or adjustable AP63200.

Critical Design Gotchas & Hardware Verification Notes

CRITICALDatasheet p.2

Pin-Identical Fixed 5.0 V vs 3.3 V Overvoltage Hazard — AP63205WU-7 ('T5') vs AP63203WU-7 ('T4')

AP63205WU-7 (marking 'T5', fixed 5.0 V) and AP63203WU-7 (marking 'T4', fixed 3.3 V) share the exact same TSOT-26 footprint, 1.1 MHz PFM clock, and direct FB-to-VOUT wiring (Fig. 1 p.2, Table p.16). Accidentally populating AP63205WU-7 onto a 3.3 V AP63203 board immediately drives 5.0 V (+51.5%) into 3.3 V logic; populating it onto an adjustable AP63200 divider (0.8 V VFB) causes severe overvoltage.

CRITICALDatasheet p.1

Scrambled Diodes TSOT-26 Pinout Completely Incompatible With TI SOT-23-6 Bucks & AP62200/AP62300

AP63205WU-7 assigns 1=FB, 2=EN, 3=VIN, 4=GND, 5=SW, 6=BST (p.1)—completely incompatible with TI's TPS54202/TPS54302/TPS563201 and Diodes' own AP62200/AP62300 (which all use 1=GND, 2=SW, 3=VIN, 4=FB, 5=EN, 6=BST). Never cross-populate across these two pinout families without a PCB respin.

WARNINGDatasheet p.4

Tight 1.0 V Margin Below 6.0 V FB Abs Max When Tied Directly to 5.0 V Rail

On fixed 5.0 V AP63205WU-7, Pin 1 (FB) ties directly to the 5.0 V VOUT rail (Fig. 1 p.2), leaving only 1.0 V of headroom below VFB Abs Max (+6.0 V, p.4). Protect the 5 V output against inductive load-dump overshoot or back-driving from external USB/5 V adapters.

INFODatasheet p.13

1.1 MHz PFM Light-Load Burst Mode & Frequency Difference vs 500 kHz AP63200/AP63201

AP63205WU-7 runs at 1.1 MHz with ±6% FSS and enters PFM below ~450 mA peak inductor current (p.10), achieving ~80% efficiency at 1 mA and ~88% at 5 mA at the expense of slightly higher PFM ripple (and +1–3% light-load VOUT uplift, Fig. 6 p.6). It uses a 4.7 uH inductor (vs 10 uH on 500 kHz AP63200/AP63201 for 5 V out, Table 1 & 3 p.13).
