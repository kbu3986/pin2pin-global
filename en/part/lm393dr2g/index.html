Engineering Overview

The onsemi LM393DR2G is a commercial-grade (0°C to +70°C) dual open-collector voltage comparator in SOIC-8 (CASE 751, PD = 570 mW, RθJA = 175.4 °C/W) rated for 2.0 V to 36 V single-supply operation (±18 V split) with 2000 V HBM / 750 V CDM ESD (Rev. 35). Delivering 1.3 us typical response time (300 ns TTL swing), ±5 mV max offset, 20 nA typ bias current, and 200 uA/ch typ Iq, it serves as a high-confidence second source to TI LM393DR, while remaining strictly incompatible with pin-identical push-pull op-amps (LM358DR/LM358DR2G) due to its uncompensated open-collector output stage.

Critical Design Gotchas & Hardware Verification Notes

CRITICALDatasheet p.1

Identical SOIC-8 Pinout Trap vs Push-Pull LM358DR2G / LM358DR Op-Amps

LM393DR2G shares the exact 8-pin SOIC layout of dual op-amps LM358DR2G, LM358DR, and LMV358IDR, but is an uncompensated open-collector comparator—NEVER swap it into an op-amp socket. Without external pull-ups its high state floats, and closing linear negative feedback triggers multi-MHz oscillation.

CRITICALDatasheet p.2

Mandatory External Pull-Up Resistor & Destructive Short-to-VCC Hazard (20 mA Max Sink)

Outputs (Pins 1 and 7) are open-collector NPN transistors guaranteeing ISink >= 6.0 mA min (16 mA typ at VOL <= 1.5 V; 20 mA abs max). While output short-to-ground is continuous, Note 1 (p.2) and Note 3 (p.3) warn that shorting an output directly to VCC causes destructive overheating. Pull-up resistance sets both rise time and logic-high level (up to 36 V).

WARNINGDatasheet p.5

Negative Input Below -0.3 V Risks Substrate Conduction & Stray-Capacitance Oscillation

Valid common-mode range is 0 V to VCC - 1.5 V at 25°C (VCC - 2.0 V over 0–70°C; only one input must stay in range while the other can exceed VCC, Note 8 p.3), but pulling EITHER input below -0.3 V violates ratings and risks output malfunction. Page 5 also warns to avoid routing OUT parallel to IN- and add <10 mV positive-feedback hysteresis.

INFODatasheet p.2

Commercial 0°C–70°C Rating & Zero Surge Margin Above 36 V (Upgraded 2 kV HBM ESD in Rev. 35)

Unlike onsemi's 250 V HBM LM358DR2G, Rev. 35 (Nov 2026) rates LM393DR2G at 2000 V HBM / 750 V CDM ESD (p.2), making it a strong pin-for-pin second source to TI LM393DR (175.4 °C/W vs TI 148.5 °C/W). For -40°C industrial designs, select LM2903DR2G (-40..105°C) or LM2903VDR2G (-40..125°C).
