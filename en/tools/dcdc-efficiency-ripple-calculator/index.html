Switching Topology Equations

  • Buck: D = Vout/(Vin·η) · ΔIL = Vout·(Vin−Vout)/(Vin·fsw·L)
  • Boost: D = 1−(Vin·η)/Vout · IL,avg = Iout·Vout/(Vin·η)
  • Buck-Boost Transition (0.85–1.15): D = Vout/(Vin+Vout) · ΔIL = Vin·D/(fsw·L)
  • Peak / DCM: Ipeak = IL,avg + ΔIL/2 · DCM when IL,avg < ΔIL/2
  • Inductor Isat Rating ≥ 1.25 × Ipeak (worst-case Vin,min)

Watch out on Boost converters: datasheets typically specify peak switch current limit (Isw), not continuous output current. At low Vin, average inductor current scales up by Vout / (Vin · η).
