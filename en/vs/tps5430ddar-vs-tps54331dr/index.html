Swap Risks & Mandatory Bench Checks

  • CRITICAL PACKAGE MISMATCH: TPS5430DDAR uses SOIC-8E (8-pin, DDA (8-Pin HSOIC PowerPAD)) while TPS54331DR uses SOIC-8 (8-pin, D); PCB footprint redesign is mandatory.
  • CRITICAL PINOUT CONFLICT: Incompatible pin assignments on Pin 2 (NC vs VIN), Pin 3 (NC vs EN), Pin 4 (FB vs SS), Pin 5 (EN vs FB), Pin 6 (GND vs COMP), Pin 7 (VIN vs GND). Soldering TPS54331DR onto a TPS5430DDAR footprint (or vice versa) without rerouting copper can short VIN/VOUT/GND and permanently damage the board.
  • Exposed thermal pad/tab mismatch (SOIC-8E: GND vs SOIC-8: NONE); PCB land pattern & solder-mask change required.
  • CRITICAL SWITCH-VOLTAGE OVERSTRESS RISK: TPS54331DR is rated up to only 25 V output (p.21) vs TPS5430DDAR up to 31.3 V (p.5); exceeding the internal switch BVdss ceiling destroys the SW MOSFET.
  • HIGH MINIMUM OUTPUT VOLTAGE FLOOR: TPS5430DDAR cannot regulate below 1.221 V (p.5) vs TPS54331DR down to 0.8 V (p.1); verify your target rail sits above the higher Vout,min floor.
  • HIGH VREF MISMATCH: Feedback reference differs (TPS5430DDAR: 1.221 V p.5 vs TPS54331DR: 0.8 V p.5); dropping in without recalculating R1/R2 will output the wrong voltage rail.
  • CAUTION INDUCTOR RETUNING: Typical inductor differs (TPS5430DDAR: 15 µH p.14 vs TPS54331DR: 6.8 µH p.16); check inductor Isat against peak switch current limit.
  • HIGH INPUT VOLTAGE RANGE SHIFT: Recommended Vin,max differs (TPS5430DDAR: 36 V p.4 vs TPS54331DR: 28 V p.4); verify line transients stay below Vin,abs,max.
  • HIGH ABSOLUTE-MAX SURGE MARGIN: TPS54331DR has a lower Vin,abs,max rating (30 V, p.4) than TPS5430DDAR (40 V, p.4); verify hot-plug, inductive kickback, and TVS clamp peaks stay safely below 30 V.
  • HIGH THERMAL MARGIN: Package θJA is 175% higher on TPS54331DR (TPS5430DDAR: 42.3 °C/W p.4 vs TPS54331DR: 116.3 °C/W p.5); re-check Tj = Ta + Pd × θJA against Tj,max.
  • HIGH MINIMUM SUPPLY FLOOR: TPS5430DDAR requires Vin,min >= 5.5 V (p.4) vs 3.5 V on the counterpart; confirm low-battery / brown-out operation still clears the higher floor.
  • HIGH TEMPERATURE GRADE MISMATCH: Rated ambient range differs (TPS5430DDAR: -40..125 °C Extended Industrial (-40°C to +125°C TJ) vs TPS54331DR: -40..150 °C Extended Industrial (-40°C to 150°C TJ)); TPS5430DDAR hot limit only 125 °C — verify product ambient qualification before swapping.

Engineering Verdict

TPS5430DDAR (Texas Instruments, SOIC-8E) and TPS54331DR (Texas Instruments, SOIC-8) are NOT drop-in interchangeable (Level 3 PCB Layout / Circuit Change Required) due to package/footprint mismatch; pinout mismatch on Pin 2 (NC vs VIN), Pin 3 (NC vs EN), Pin 4 (FB vs SS), Pin 5 (EN vs FB), Pin 6 (GND vs COMP), Pin 7 (VIN vs GND); exposed pad / thermal tab net mismatch. Specific pin conflicts: Pin 2 (NC vs VIN), Pin 3 (NC vs EN), Pin 4 (FB vs SS), Pin 5 (EN vs FB). Power-stage deltas: buck (non-sync) vs buck (non-sync), switching frequency 500 kHz (p.5) vs 570 kHz (p.6) (1.1x ratio), typical inductor 15 µH (p.14) vs 6.8 µH (p.16), feedback Vref 1.221 V (p.5) vs 0.8 V (p.5), and package θJA 42.3 vs 116.3 °C/W. Primary bench gotchas to watch: TPS5430DDAR "7 V Abs Max ENA Pin Trap & Float-to-Enable — Tying ENA to 12 V/24 V VIN Destroys TPS5430DDAR" (p.4) and TPS54331DR "Non-Synchronous Architecture Requires External Schottky Catch Diode & External COMP Network" (p.1). BOM decision: Choose TPS5430DDAR when bus voltage exceeds 28 V (rated to 36 V) or programmed output rails exceed 25 V (up to 31.3 V); choose TPS54331DR when light-load battery standby needs 110 µA Iq. Always verify Pin 1 orientation (TOP_VIEW p.3 vs TOP_VIEW p.3) and official datasheets on hardware prototypes prior to production.
