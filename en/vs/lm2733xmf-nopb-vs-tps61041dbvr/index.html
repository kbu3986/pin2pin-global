Swap Risks & Mandatory Bench Checks

  • CRITICAL SWITCH-VOLTAGE OVERSTRESS RISK: TPS61041DBVR is rated up to only 28 V output (p.4) vs LM2733XMF/NOPB up to 38 V (p.3); exceeding the internal switch BVdss ceiling destroys the SW MOSFET.
  • HIGH MINIMUM OUTPUT VOLTAGE FLOOR: LM2733XMF/NOPB cannot regulate below 5 V (p.12) vs TPS61041DBVR down to 1.8 V (p.5); verify your target rail sits above the higher Vout,min floor.
  • HIGH SWITCHING FREQUENCY SHIFT: fsw differs by 38% (LM2733XMF/NOPB: 1600 kHz p.5 vs TPS61041DBVR: 1000 kHz p.4); re-evaluate inductor core loss, ripple current ΔIL, output capacitor ESR ripple, and EMI harmonics.
  • HIGH INPUT VOLTAGE RANGE SHIFT: Recommended Vin,max differs (LM2733XMF/NOPB: 14 V p.4 vs TPS61041DBVR: 6 V p.4); verify line transients stay below Vin,abs,max.
  • CAUTION CURRENT RATING BASIS: LM2733XMF/NOPB specifies 1000 mA (switch current limit Isw, p.4) vs TPS61041DBVR 250 mA (switch current limit Isw, p.5). Remember boost/switch-limited Iout,max is strictly less than Isw due to duty-cycle and inductor ripple scaling.
  • HIGH ABSOLUTE-MAX SURGE MARGIN: TPS61041DBVR has a lower Vin,abs,max rating (7 V, p.4) than LM2733XMF/NOPB (14.5 V, p.3); verify hot-plug, inductive kickback, and TVS clamp peaks stay safely below 7 V.
  • HIGH MINIMUM SUPPLY FLOOR: LM2733XMF/NOPB requires Vin,min >= 2.7 V (p.4) vs 1.8 V on the counterpart; confirm low-battery / brown-out operation still clears the higher floor.
  • HIGH TEMPERATURE GRADE MISMATCH: Rated ambient range differs (LM2733XMF/NOPB: -40..125 °C Industrial / Extended (-40°C to 125°C TJ) vs TPS61041DBVR: -40..85 °C Industrial (-40°C to 85°C TA, TJ max 125°C)); TPS61041DBVR hot limit only 85 °C — verify product ambient qualification before swapping.

Engineering Verdict

LM2733XMF/NOPB (Texas Instruments, SOT-23-5) and TPS61041DBVR (Texas Instruments, SOT-23-5) are Level 2 Functional / Tuning Required alternatives: the PCB land pattern is compatible, but passive retuning or rating derating is mandatory because adjustable Vout,max ceiling drops >=20% (28 V vs 38 V — switch-voltage overstress risk); minimum programmable Vout,min rises (1.8 V vs 5 V); switching frequency differs >30%. Power-stage deltas: boost (non-sync) vs boost (non-sync), switching frequency 1600 kHz (p.5) vs 1000 kHz (p.4) (1.6x ratio), typical inductor 10 µH (p.1) vs 10 µH (p.4), feedback Vref 1.23 V (p.4) vs 1.233 V (p.5), and package θJA 210 vs 205.2 °C/W. Primary bench gotchas to watch: LM2733XMF/NOPB "Mandatory Feedforward Capacitor Cf Across R1 Prevents Loop Oscillation" (p.13) and TPS61041DBVR "Lower 250 mA Switch Limit (215 mA Min) & Higher 750 mOhm RDS(on) vs Pin-Identical TPS61040DBVR" (p.5). BOM decision: Choose LM2733XMF/NOPB when bus voltage exceeds 6 V (rated to 14 V) or higher switch current capacity (1000 mA) is required; choose TPS61041DBVR when light-load battery standby needs 28 µA Iq. Always verify Pin 1 orientation (TOP_VIEW p.3 vs TOP_VIEW p.3) and official datasheets on hardware prototypes prior to production.
