Does the Pin2Pin BOM checker upload files?

No. CSV parsing, OPN lookup, and alternate matching execute in browser memory with zero server uploads.

What do Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 pinout grades mean?

Level 1 matches package and pin roles as a candidate drop-in. Level 2 needs passive or thermal tuning. Level 3 flags pin conflicts requiring PCB changes.

Which semiconductor families are in the database?

Verified LDO regulators, buck and boost DC-DC converters, op-amps, and comparators from official PDFs. Large 1,000-page MCUs stay out of scope.

How do I report a pinout or datasheet error?

Submit the official PDF URL, revision, and page number via error reporting, and review our engineering disclaimer.
