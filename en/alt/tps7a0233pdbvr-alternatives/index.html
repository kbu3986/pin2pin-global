Baseline Target Reference (TPS7A0233PDBVR)

The Texas Instruments TPS7A0233PDBVR is a nanopower 200 mA fixed 3.3 V (±1.5% over -40°C to +125°C) LDO in a 5-pin DBV SOT-23-5 package (1=IN, 2=GND, 3=EN active high with smart pulldown, 4=NC, 5=OUT with 60 Ohm active discharge). Operating from 1.5 V to 6.0 V (6.5 V abs max), it achieves a benchmark 25 nA (0.025 uA) quiescent current even in dropout and 3 nA shutdown current, with <= 270 mV max dropout at 200 mA (to 85°C), 55 dB PSRR at 1 kHz, and 181.9 °C/W RθJA. Designers must respect its 22 uF maximum derated output capacitance cap, 1.2–1.6 ms startup time, and lack of reverse-current blocking.

Pre-Production Substitution Verification Checklist

  • Verify exact pin numbering on SOT-23-5 (1=VIN, 2=GND, 3=EN, 4=NC, 5=VOUT) and exposed pad net (None).
  • Confirm output capacitor ESR stability (MLCC stable) and DC-bias derated capacitance >= 1 µF.
  • Check worst-case junction temperature Tj = Ta + (Vin,max - 3.3 V) × Iout × θJA stays >= 15 °C below Tj,max (150 °C).
  • Validate input transient headroom against Vin,max (6 V rec / 6.5 V abs) and Pin 4 (NC vs BYPASS) PCB net connectivity.
